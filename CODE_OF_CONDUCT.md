# Code of Conduct

CricketVerse Arena is a friendly project. We want everyone who plays, reports bugs, or contributes code to feel welcome.

## Our standards

Please:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to programming.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Accept that people disagree, and keep discussions focused on what is best for the project.
- Credit other people's work.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or language (race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, nationality, age, or anything else).
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, spamming, or deliberately derailing conversations.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code review) and anywhere you represent the project, including in-game chat on servers run by the maintainers.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/nick10-05/cricketverse/security/advisories/new) or via the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. Usually that means a private warning first; repeated or serious problems lead to a ban.
